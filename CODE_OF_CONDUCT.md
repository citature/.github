# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the citature project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

Because citature works with records about real people and organizations, one
addition is specific to this project: do not use the project's issues,
discussions, or tools to target, profile, or build a case against a private
individual. citature is entity-level by design and the community follows the
same line the product does.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via a private
[security advisory](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)
on this repository. Maintainers will review and respond as promptly as they can,
and will respect the reporter's privacy.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of
Conduct, and may ban contributors for behavior they judge inappropriate,
threatening, or harmful.
