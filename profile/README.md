**Agent-native tools over public US federal records.**

### The data &nbsp;·&nbsp; primary sources only

Public-domain US federal records, refreshed nightly from the source of record —
never an aggregator.

- **FEC** — campaign finance: 217M+ itemized contribution records across the
  2020–2026 cycles, plus committees and candidates
- **USASpending** — federal awards and contracts, keyed on recipient UEI
- **SAM.gov** — contract opportunities and solicitations
- **Grants.gov** — grant opportunities

### The tools &nbsp;·&nbsp; on [Apify Store](https://apify.com/citature)

- **Money** — FEC contributions by state and committee type, federal awards by
  agency/state/recipient, follow-the-money committee inflows, and funding-entity
  lookup and search
- **GovCon** — expiring-contract recompetes by NAICS with incumbent UEI,
  contractor award lineage by agency, and agency buying patterns by year
- **MCP** — every tool above delivered as agent-native calls from one MCP server

### The rules

Research over public records — never legal, financial, or eligibility advice.
Entity-level data only; no individual-person products. citature reports what the
record says and never renders a verdict.
