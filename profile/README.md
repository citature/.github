## citature

**Answers with receipts.** Every citature tool returns a structured answer plus
the receipt behind it: citations to the exact upstream government source,
retrieval timestamps, coverage caveats, and match provenance on any
cross-source join.

### The data

Public-domain US federal records, refreshed nightly from primary sources only —
never aggregators.

- **FEC** — campaign finance: 217M+ itemized contribution records across the
  2020–2026 cycles, committees, candidates
- **USASpending** — federal awards and contracts, keyed on recipient UEI
- **SAM.gov** — contract opportunities and solicitations
- **Grants.gov** — grant opportunities

### The tools

Available on [Apify Store](https://apify.com/citature):

- **Money** — FEC contributions by state and committee type, federal awards by
  agency/state/recipient, follow-the-money committee inflows, funding-entity
  lookup and search
- **GovCon** — expiring-contract recompetes by NAICS with incumbent UEI,
  contractor award lineage by agency, agency buying patterns by year
- **MCP** — every answer available as agent-native tools from one MCP server

### The rules

Research over public records — never legal, financial, or eligibility advice.
Entity-level data only; no individual-person products. citature reports what
the record says and never renders a verdict.
